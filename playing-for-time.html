Telephoning: Playing for Time
Worksheet TE10 · Part B

Sometimes on a phone call you need to fill the silence while you look for information. These phrases keep the conversation going and sound professional while you search for what you need.

💡

These expressions are used across many telephoning situations: when you need to check a file, find a date in your diary, or confirm a detail before answering. Using them confidently makes you sound natural and in control — even when you're not quite ready to answer yet.
